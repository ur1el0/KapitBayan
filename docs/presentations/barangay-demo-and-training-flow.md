# Barangay demo and user-training flow

**Audience:** Barangay administrators, office staff, field officials, and residents

**Suggested session:** 45–50 minutes

**Printable copy:** [Barangay demo and training flow (PDF)](barangay-demo-and-training-flow.pdf)

Use this guide to show people where to go, what to do, and what happens next. Follow one sample resident request through the system, then let each group try the part they use. Button names can vary slightly between web and mobile releases.

## Before people arrive

- Prepare a separate demo sign-in for a barangay administrator, a field official, and a resident. The system uses assigned account roles; do not share one person's password with the group.
- Use a designated demo barangay and made-up resident details. Do not project real PhilSys images, addresses, phone numbers, payment details, reset links, or passwords.
- Approve the demo resident before the session if you plan to demonstrate protected resident services. Otherwise, show the pending-verification screen and explain what it means.
- Check that the website, mobile app, and demo accounts point to the same test barangay and backend. Keep a screenshot or short recording ready in case the network is unavailable.
- Prepare one accurate sample notice, activity, hotline, document request, and queue ticket. Verify names, dates, locations, and numbers before using them.
- Do not create or delete records in a live barangay account unless the barangay has agreed that the item is demo data and approved its cleanup.
- For older residents or first-time app users, allow extra time, increase phone text size if helpful, and teach one screen at a time.

## The story for the session

Show the same service from each person's point of view:

**Resident submits or checks a request → barangay staff review and act → the resident sees the updated status or notice.**

Explain that the system shares information and tracks work. Staff still make decisions, verify documents and payments, and contact residents when needed. The mobile app needs an internet connection for server-backed services.

| Time | Who | What to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 min | Everyone | Explain the resident, office, and field workflows |
| 5 min | Resident + administrator | Registration and account verification |
| 8 min | Resident + office staff | Submit and process a document request |
| 5 min | Resident + queue staff | Get a ticket, call the next resident, and view the update |
| 5 min | Resident + office staff | Submit and review an assistance or issue report |
| 5 min | Administrator + resident | Notices, activities, hotlines, and FAQs |
| 5 min | Resident on mobile | Main navigation, profile, and help |
| 4 min | Field official | Queue ticker, clearance check, and field reports |
| 4–9 min | Everyone | Notification and password-reset fallbacks, questions, and practice |

## 1. Orient the group

**Say:** “Residents use KapitBayan to find information and send service requests. Barangay staff review and process those requests. Field officials use the mobile tools assigned to their work. Everyone signs in with their own account.”

Point out the correct sign-in before opening any account. An `ADMIN` account gets the barangay office web workspace. A `FIELD_OFFICIAL` account gets the field mobile workspace. A resident account gets the resident web portal or resident mobile screens. The mobile `ADMIN` dashboard shows an overview and supported operations; use the web workspace for the full set of office-management pages.

## 2. Show resident registration and approval

1. On the resident web sign-in page, choose **Register as Resident**. On mobile, start at **Register**.
2. Explain that the resident enters their own account and barangay information and submits the requested registration evidence. Do not use a real resident's identity document in a public demo.
3. Sign in to the barangay web workspace. In the left menu, open **Residents**, then **Resident Verifications**.
4. Open the prepared demo record. Show where staff review it and make an approval or rejection decision based on the barangay's process.
5. Return to the resident sign-in. After approval, the resident can sign in and use protected services. If it is still pending, the resident must wait for an authorized review; creating another account is not the fix.

**Explain:** Registration creates an account request. It does not automatically verify the resident or approve any separate document or aid request.

## 3. Follow a document request from resident to staff

### Resident

1. On the web, open **Documents & Clearances**. On mobile, tap **Documents** in the bottom navigation.
2. Choose the requested document, complete the form, review the details, and submit.
3. Return to **Documents** later to see the request and its current status.

### Barangay office staff

1. In the web workspace, choose **Documents** from the left menu and open the prepared request.
2. Review the request and evidence. Record the supported processing decision, fee assessment, or waiver, then update the request through the available controls.
3. Explain that a resident pays only when the request reaches the applicable payment-ready state. Staff manually check cash or transfer evidence. An uploaded receipt or transfer reference is evidence; it is not automatic proof that funds settled. Required Official Receipt controls still apply before release.
4. Return to the resident screen and refresh the request to show the status change.

**Say:** “KapitBayan records the steps. It does not decide whether a resident qualifies, move money, or verify a bank transfer automatically.” Do not collect real money or show a real receipt during training.

## 4. Demonstrate the live queue

### Resident

- On the web, open **Queue** and choose **Get Queue Ticket**. Follow the form and keep the ticket number visible.
- In the mobile app, tap **Queue** on the bottom bar, then use **Get Ticket**. The ticket and serving status are shown on this screen.
- Residents can also use the barangay's public queue display where it is available.

### Queue staff

1. In the web workspace, open **Queue**. Point out the currently served ticket, waiting list, and queue metrics.
2. Use **Manual Entry** only for the prepared walk-in demo. Resident tickets and staff-entered walk-ins appear in the queue for staff to serve.
3. Choose **Next Queue** to call the next waiting ticket. Choose **Mark as Done** when the service is complete.
4. If demonstrating **Page resident**, use the prepared test resident and explain that the alert is saved in the resident's in-app notification center. Email and phone push are additional delivery attempts; they may not display immediately.

**Expected result:** The staff queue and resident ticket show the same serving progress after refresh. If either view is stale, check the connection and reload before creating a second ticket.

## 5. Handle assistance and issue reports

### Resident

1. On the web, open **Assistance**. In mobile, open **Quick Services → Request Aid**.
2. Choose the request type, add the requested details, and submit. Return to the same page to check the status and any staff note.
3. To report a local issue in mobile, open **Quick Services → Report Issue**, complete the description and urgency, and submit. Use the barangay's approved hotline for immediate danger; the report form is not an emergency-response service.

### Barangay office or field staff

1. On the web, an authorized administrator opens **Aid Requests**, reviews the request, and records a decision with a resident-facing note. Staff decide eligibility; the system does not approve aid automatically.
2. Open **Reports** in the web workspace or **Field Reports** in the field official mobile workspace to review an issue and record a supported status or urgency update.
3. Return to the resident view to show the updated status or note.

## 6. Publish community information

1. In the barangay web workspace, open **Communications**.
2. Use the **Announcements** tab to add the prepared notice. If useful, show pinning, an image, and the share option. Sharing gives staff a link or share kit; it does not automatically publish to Facebook.
3. Use **Activity Schedules** to add a prepared activity with its title, location, date, time, and description.
4. Use **Emergency Hotlines** to add a barangay-approved contact and category.
5. Sign in as the resident. On the web, open **Bulletin** to find announcements and activities. In mobile, open **Announcements** or **Schedule**. Use the **Hotlines** quick service to view contact numbers.
6. Open **FAQs** and show a common resident question. Residents can reopen **Help & FAQs** and the **Tutorial** whenever they need them.

**Presenter note:** In the current communications screens, staff can add these records, but the screens do not provide an edit control for existing announcements, activities, or hotlines. Check all demo text and dates before saving. Do not delete an existing barangay record to correct a demo item.

## 7. Give residents a mobile navigation tour

On the resident phone, point to these places:

- **Dashboard:** service summary, recent notices, and quick-service shortcuts.
- **Queue:** request or follow a ticket.
- **Documents:** make a request and check its progress.
- **Schedule:** see community activities and dates.
- **Quick Services:** open assistance, issue reporting, hotlines, and help/FAQs.
- **Profile icon:** update available profile details and notification preferences. Residents can also open **Help & FAQs** and the **Tutorial** again later.

For the web portal, point to the top navigation: **Documents**, **Queue**, **Bulletin**, **Assistance**, and **FAQs**. The resident's **Profile & Settings** control is at the right. On a narrow screen, scroll the navigation row if some options are off screen.

Residents should never give a helper their password, one-time code, or reset link. A helper may read the screen aloud or help tap a button while the resident enters private information themselves.

For a calendar event, open its details and tap **Add to Calendar**. The phone calendar opens for the resident to review and save the event. If no calendar app opens, use the date, time, and location shown in Schedule and add them manually.

## 8. Show the field official's mobile tools

Sign in with a field-official account and open the **Field Operations** screen. Show its three tabs:

1. **Queue Ticker:** review the live queue and call the next ticket when that action is assigned to the account.
2. **Verify Clearance:** enter the prepared tracking number and show the returned status. Use made-up or approved demo records only.
3. **Field Reports:** review reports for the assigned barangay and record a supported status or urgency update.

Explain that field access is limited to the assigned barangay. If a tab or action is unavailable, use the account role and permissions actually assigned; do not switch to another person's admin credentials.

## 9. Explain notifications and what to do if one is missing

- A staff member can page a resident from the serving queue ticket. The backend saves an in-app resident alert, then separately attempts optional email and phone push according to account settings and device registration.
- On mobile, open the resident's notification center from the dashboard to see saved alerts. A push banner depends on the resident's push preference, Android notification permission, network, and a valid registered device.
- On the web, residents can manage **Email updates** in **Profile & Settings**. The web portal does not register a phone for push notifications.
- If no phone banner appears, open the app notification center and refresh; check Profile preferences and Android notification permission. Treat email and push as best-effort delivery, not guaranteed contact.
- If the saved in-app alert is missing too, check the network and sign-in, refresh the screen, then use the barangay's normal desk or contact channel while the issue is investigated.

## 10. Demonstrate password recovery

1. Return to the sign-in page and select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the demo account's email and submit once. The page shows a generic success message for privacy; it does not prove that an email was sent.
3. Check the mailbox's Inbox and Spam folder. The deployed email provider is Brevo. If needed, an authorized operator checks Render application logs and Brevo transactional email logs.
4. If the resident cannot access the registered email, ask them to contact the official account-support process. An authorized DILG support operator must verify the account holder before correcting the address or sending a recovery link. Do not ask for the resident's password or reset link.

If the account is pending verification, password reset will not approve it. If a reset email does not arrive, check that the address belongs to exactly one account and wait before retrying if the service has rate-limited requests. A resident can continue to request in-person help through the barangay office.

## 11. Practice and close

Invite one person from each group to perform one safe task using the demo account:

- **Resident:** find an FAQ, open a notice, and find the queue or document status.
- **Barangay administrator or office staff:** find the verification list or prepared request, then explain the next review step.
- **Field official:** open each assigned tab and locate the sample queue ticket or tracking number.

Close with: “Use your own account, confirm you are in the correct barangay, and check the request status before submitting again. For urgent concerns or a service outage, use the barangay's established in-person or official contact channel.”

## If something goes wrong during the demo

| What happens | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Resident login says verification is pending | Check **Residents → Resident Verifications**. The account must be reviewed by authorized barangay staff. |
| Page keeps loading or says connection failed | Check Wi-Fi/mobile data, refresh once, and use the page's retry control. Server-backed mobile features need internet. |
| Queue ticket or status looks old | Refresh both staff and resident views. Check the ticket before creating another one. |
| Push banner does not appear | Check the saved in-app notification, resident push preference, Android permission, and network. |
| Password reset says “check your inbox” but no email arrives | Check Inbox and Spam, confirm the demo email belongs to one account, then contact the service operator; do not repeatedly submit requests. |
| A hotline button cannot open the dialer | Read the approved number and enter it in the phone app manually. Do not place a real emergency call as a test. |
| Feature is missing from a sign-in | Confirm the person is using their own assigned role and the correct web or mobile app. |
