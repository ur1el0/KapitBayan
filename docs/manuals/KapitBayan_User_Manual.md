# KapitBayan User Manual

**Draft for adviser review — November 2026**
This manual describes the current web and Android workflows. Barangay staff retain authority over identity verification, required evidence, fees, payment, priority eligibility, and document release.

## 1. Getting started

Use an internet-connected browser or the Android app. Sign in with the account created for your role. Residents register their own account; a Barangay Admin or platform operator may need to review an account before all services are available. Barangay Admin and Field Official accounts are provisioned through the Barangay’s account process.

If you forget your password, use **Forgot password**, enter the email registered to the account, then open the reset message and follow its link to choose a new password. Check Spam/Junk and confirm the address if the message does not appear. The reset link is time-limited; request another one if it has expired. Do not share a reset link or password with staff.

## 2. Resident web and Android app

### Sign in and dashboard

1. Enter the resident account’s username or email and password, then sign in.
2. Read the dashboard’s newest service notices and notification banner. Use the dashboard cards and navigation to open documents, queue, reports/assistance, community schedule, hotlines, and profile.
3. Use the language control to switch between English and Filipino. The interface labels are translated where available; Barangay-authored announcements, schedules, and free-text details may remain in the language their author used.

### Request a document

1. Open **Documents** and choose a document that the Barangay has published.
2. Read the published fee, details, and per-document checklist. Check any items you already have ready to bring. This checklist is a reminder; it does not upload evidence or tell staff that the requirement has been verified.
3. Enter the purpose if requested and submit the request.
4. Follow the status shown for that request: pending, processing, ready for pickup, released, or rejected. Staff may add a note or confirm the current fee and next step.
5. Bring the listed physical requirements and payment evidence to the Barangay when instructed. For electronic payment, select the available method/recipient, submit the transfer reference and proof image, and wait for staff review. The app does not itself move money or treat a screenshot as proof of settlement.
6. The downloadable PDF available for a released request is an **O.R.-number slip**, not the issued clearance or certificate. Obtain the actual document from the Barangay’s authorized process.

### Get a queue ticket for a service visit

1. Open the queue page or use **Get counter ticket** on the document request.
2. When the visit is for a particular document request, request the counter ticket from that request so the visit stays linked to it. Only one active waiting/serving ticket is allowed for a document request.
3. Watch the ticket status on screen. Queue status refreshes periodically while the page is open; a refresh interval is not a promise that the server or phone network updates instantly.
4. When staff calls the ticket, show the ticket number at the service counter. Bring the requested documents and any O.R./payment slip the Barangay instructed you to bring. Staff checks any priority eligibility.

### Reports, assistance, schedule, and profile

- Use **Report an issue** to submit the available issue details and optional supporting information. Urgent reports should also be communicated through the Barangay’s emergency contact or appropriate emergency service; an app submission is not an emergency dispatch service.
- Use **Request assistance** to submit the available assistance request. Follow status updates and instructions from staff.
- Open the calendar/schedule and announcements for community activities. Published content is provided by Barangay staff and may not be translated automatically.
- Open **Hotlines** to view contact numbers. On Android, selecting a number opens the phone dialer; review the number before placing the call.
- In **Profile**, review your account details and notification preferences. Android push notifications require device permission and a working internet connection. In-app notices and push are not guaranteed to arrive at the same time.

## 3. Barangay Admin web and Android app

### Dashboard and notifications

Sign in using the official Admin account. Review dashboard counts and charts as operational summaries of records currently stored in the Barangay tenant. An empty chart means no matching records are available; it is not a hidden sample dataset. Use the notification bell to review new queue, document, report, or resident events.

### Residents and Field Officials

- Open **Residents** to review resident applications and verification evidence under the Barangay’s procedure. Approve or reject only after checking the evidence and the person’s details.
- For a minor, review the linked guardian account and supporting evidence in the resident dossier before approval. The system records the Admin’s review action; it does not independently establish legal guardianship.
- Use staff management to invite/create, edit, activate, or deactivate assigned Field Official accounts. A Field Official sets their own password through the email link. Deactivation is performed by the Admin.
- Bulk resident import uses the provided CSV template. Check the import summary for accepted, skipped, or invalid rows.

### Document services and requests

1. Open **Documents** and manage the Barangay’s document service catalog.
2. Add or edit each document’s name, applicable common requirements (checkboxes), any additional service-specific requirements, published fee, and instructions. Select only requirements confirmed for that document; the common options are not universal rules. Save the offering to publish the current details to residents. New requests use a snapshot of those details; editing the catalog does not silently rewrite earlier applications.
3. Open a request to review the resident’s purpose and submitted evidence. Record the processing status and staff notes. Confirm final fee, waiver/exemption basis, payment, O.R. number, pickup, and release according to Barangay procedure.
4. Electronic payment proofs are manually reviewed. Payment verification, fee assessment, waivers/exemptions, and O.R. recording are restricted to Barangay Admins in the merged revision. Confirm the production deployment before relying on the updated role behavior.
5. Walk-in requests can be entered by authorized staff. Explain the required items and next step to the resident before they leave.
6. Once the QR feature is deployed, after a paper certificate has actually been issued and the request is marked **Released**, an Admin can confirm issuance and print a separate verification QR label to attach to the paper. The QR checks the Barangay's issuance record; it does not replace or generate the paper certificate.

### Queue, issues, aid, and communications

- In **Queue**, view waiting residents, call the next ticket, mark service complete, and enter walk-ins as allowed. Grant priority only after staff confirms the applicable eligibility. The public Now Serving board shows ticket numbers without resident names.
- Review issue reports and aid requests, assign or update status, and communicate the next step. Use the designated emergency process for urgent events.
- In **Communications**, publish announcements, activity schedules, and emergency hotlines. Check date, time, location, and contact details before publishing. Share the generated public link/QR or caption only after reviewing the content.
- Maintain FAQs based on questions residents actually ask. Answers should reflect current Barangay policy.
- Use the account sign-in history for the account-monitoring purpose shown in Settings. It records the timestamp, browser/app user-agent, and optional IP address for successful sign-ins and retains those events for 90 days. It is not GPS tracking, and an IP address alone does not prove a person’s physical location.

## 4. Field Official / Tanod Android app

1. Sign in using the official account and password set through the invitation/reset email.
2. Use the assigned queue and field-service pages to review the ticket or issue assigned to you and record the permitted service outcome.
3. Check the ticket number, linked document request, and O.R. slip with the resident when the workflow requires it. Report mismatches to the Barangay Admin; do not approve payment, change fees, grant waivers, or record an O.R. number.
4. Use **Forgot password** if you cannot sign in. The reset message is sent to the email assigned to your account. Ask the Barangay Admin to check whether your account is active if the link works but sign-in is rejected.

## 5. Barangay Operator

1. Sign in to the operator account and open **Barangay Applications**.
2. Review the submitted locality, proposed Admin, email verification, attestations, and proof-of-authority document using the secure review action.
3. Independently verify the applicant’s identity and the Barangay through an official channel. The uploaded form is supporting evidence, not proof by itself.
4. Approve only after the required checks and attestations are complete, or reject with a clear reason. Approval creates/activates the Barangay’s Admin access; it does not give the operator access to resident service records.
5. Use account support only for permitted recovery/deactivation workflows. Never ask a user to send a password or reset token.

## 6. If something fails

- **A page shows an error:** use its Retry/Refresh control once and confirm the connection. Avoid repeating a create-request action until you can check whether the first request was saved.
- **No password-reset email:** check Spam/Junk and that you entered the email associated with the account. If it still does not arrive, contact the account’s Barangay Admin or platform operator through the official support path.
- **A push notification is missing:** open the app and check its notification/request list, confirm Android notification permission and the app’s push preference, and check internet access. Push delivery depends on Firebase and the device; it is not guaranteed.
- **No network:** the mobile app may show clearly marked saved copies of announcements, hotlines, document requests, queue tickets, aid requests, and issue reports. They may be out of date; a saved ticket does not show a live queue position. Submissions are not queued or sent offline. After a timeout, reconnect and check the corresponding history before trying again.
- **The O.R. PDF is not the certificate:** it is only the O.R.-number slip. Contact the Barangay for the actual clearance/certificate and its authenticity-verification process.

## 7. Privacy and safe use

Use only your assigned account. Do not share passwords, reset links, payment images, IDs, or another resident’s records. Review identity and payment evidence only when your role permits it. Use the Barangay’s approved retention and privacy notice for resident documents, especially evidence involving minors. Log out on shared devices.

## 8. Known scope limits

- The Android app is configured for API 24 (Android 7.0) and later. iOS is not built.
- The mobile client provides encrypted, read-only offline snapshots for announcements, hotlines, and up to 100 records per resident request list. It does not cache evidence images or queue offline writes.
- Queue and notification refresh use periodic HTTP requests, not WebSockets/SSE.
- Browser compatibility and real-device notification delivery must be verified and documented before claiming tested support.
- Certificate-verification QR creation and lookup code is included in merged PR #317, but production create/lookup behavior and a real print/scan check have not yet been verified. The pending `0021` migration is for request/ticket/receipt uniqueness and is not the QR model migration.
- A public self-service kiosk and online payment processing are not described as available features in this manual.
